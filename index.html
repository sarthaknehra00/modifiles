Editorial & Evidentiary Methodology

1. The Tripartite Standard

For every contested claim in modern Indian political discourse, this archive maintains the Claim • Evidence • Response structure. This ensures the viewer can scrutinize the opposition's allegations, examine verified factual archives, and review official government rebuttals and conclusive judicial rulings together on the same screen.

2. Legal & Judicial Distinctions

Where matters have reached final judicial resolution in the Supreme Court of India (such as the 2002 SIT Clean Chit upholding in Zakia Jafri or the Article 370 abrogation validity), they are clearly identified as concluded legal jurisprudence. Where litigation is ongoing (such as the Gujarat University degree defamation trial or the US federal court indictment concerning Adani Group executives), proceedings are strictly demarcated as open litigation rather than settled factual determinations.

3. Primary Source Discipline

Every chapter integrates primary documents: Election Commission affidavits, university registrar notifications, gazette statutes, court transcripts, Reserve Bank of India annual reports, and verified photographic archives.
